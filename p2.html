Quiz Prático de Business English

Restaurantes corporativos, competências, projetos em andamento e o guia definitivo para dominar TO BE vs. DO/DOES.

Resumo Essencial

Desmistificando TO BE (am/is/are) vs. DO / DOES

1. Quando usar TO BE (am / is / are)

  • Profissões, cargos e identidade: I am an export manager. / Is she the boss?
  • Estados, características e sentimentos: He is reliable. / Are you ready? / They aren't late.
  • Lugares e localização: Where are your offices? / The fair is in Atlanta.
  • Auxiliar do Present Continuous (ação agora / projeto em curso): Are you working on the budget? / We are decorating the office.

2. Quando usar DO / DOES (Simple Present)

  • Perguntas e negativas com verbos de ação (rotinas e fatos): work, live, export, have, like, travel, deal, report.
  • Eu / Você / Nós / Eles (I, you, we, they): use do / don't. Do you work in Lisbon? / We don't have time.
  • Ele / Ela / Empresa (he, she, it): use does / doesn't + verbo sem -s. Does she travel a lot? / He doesn't like paperwork.
  • Regra de Ouro: O verbo principal fica na forma base (sem -ing e sem -s).
Erros clássicos para nunca mais cometer:
ERRADO "Are you work here?" → CERTO "Do you work here?" (rotina) ou "Are you working here?" (agora)
ERRADO "Do you are the manager?" → CERTO "Are you the manager?" (TO BE nunca usa DO/DOES)
ERRADO "Where do you working?" → CERTO "Where are you working?" (verbo com -ING exige TO BE)
